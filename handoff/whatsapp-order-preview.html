What this is

  • One tap to open the whole order — brief, ledger and photos as a second page.
  • Reads like a proper booking confirmation, easy to forward to a helper or supplier.
  • The text inside the PDF isn't searchable from WhatsApp's chat search.
  • Needs a small build step to turn each submission into a formatted PDF (or a Google Doc from a template) before it sends.

Weighing it up

Same order, two formats. This is the actual trade-off — not a recommendation either way.

 As messagesAs a document
Opening itReads immediately, no tapOne tap to open the PDF
Finding it againSearchable in WhatsApp chat searchLives inside the file, not searchable from chat
Forwarding to someone elseSeveral messages to forward togetherOne file, forwards in one go
Reference photosFull resolution, swipeable in the chatBundled onto page 2 of the PDF
First impressionReads like a very organised customer texted youReads like a booking confirmation or invoice
To build (Phase 2)Text + image messages via the WhatsApp Business APISame API, plus a PDF-from-template step before sending
