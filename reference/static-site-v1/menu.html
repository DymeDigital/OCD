Leaf I — The Flavours

Fourteen entries,
each perfected once.

Every flavour below is logged exactly as it leaves the kitchen. All cakes are finished in white chocolate ganache unless the entry says otherwise.

Most Requested

Plate I

Belgian Chocolate, Chocolate Ganache Filling

A rich, moist chocolate sponge made with premium Belgian cocoa, layered generously with silky chocolate ganache to a luscious, fudge-like finish.

Plate II

Vanilla Bean, Caramel Ganache Filling

A butter-based sponge infused with real vanilla bean, filled with golden caramel ganache. Swiss meringue filling optional.

Plate III

Lemon & Blueberry, Cream Cheese Filling

Juicy blueberries and bright lemon in a butter-based sponge, layered with a tangy cream cheese filling.

Plate IV

Toasted Nut, Cream Cheese Filling

Toasted coconut, almonds, and pecans in a rich buttery cake, balanced with a tangy cream cheese filling.

Plate VII

Salted Caramel

Plate VIII
