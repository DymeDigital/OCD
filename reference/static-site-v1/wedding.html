Wedding cakes are bespoke by nature and kept under a separate Wedding Cake Contract, with its own design lock-in and cancellation terms. This entry starts that conversation — a formal design and quote follow once we've spoken.

Terms of This Entry — Wedding Cake Contract

BookingSecured by a completed inquiry and full payment; orders are not confirmed until payment is received and confirmed by us.
Design lock-inDesign, themes, colours and inspiration images are supplied at booking. Changes requested less than 14 days before the event may incur additional charges and are subject to feasibility.
Guest countAn accurate guest count and cake requirement must be confirmed at least 14 days before the wedding.
PaymentFull payment confirms booking; 50% serves as a non-refundable deposit.
CancellationsMore than 14 days before the event — deposit forfeited, no further charge. Within 14 days — 75% cancellation fee. Within 5 days — non-refundable, no credit. Otherwise, a credit voucher valid 36 months is issued per notice period.
Delivery & setupWe deliver and set up at the venue within an agreed window; charges are distance-based and quoted separately. You or a representative must supervise the cake on delivery.
AllergensIngredients are disclosed but allergen-free products cannot be guaranteed. Please disclose dietary needs at least 14 days before the event.
Governing lawSouth African law; disputes are resolved in the courts of Durban.
