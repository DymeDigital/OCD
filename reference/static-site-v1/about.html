A business run
like a ledger.

Obsessive Cupcake Disorder (OCD) is a Durban cake and confection studio built on a simple admission: nothing here is left to approximation. The name is the joke; the standard behind it isn't.

Every petal on this finish was piped and placed by hand — the kind of detail that doesn't photograph as "obsessive," just as right.

What obsession looks like in the kitchen

Premium Belgian cocoa and Callebaut chocolate, real vanilla bean, fourteen flavours held to a written description precise enough to be a recipe in itself. Cake sizes are never guessed at — they're advised against your exact serving count, because a size chart is a starting point, not a rule that overrides your event.

What obsession looks like after it leaves

Every cake ships with the same instructions, followed exactly: lifted from the base only, kept level through transport and refrigeration, driven directly with no harsh braking. Left too cold, a butter-based sponge turns dense and a ganache finish can crack — so storage and serving temperature are treated as part of the recipe, not an afterthought.
